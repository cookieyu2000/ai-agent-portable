# Third-party sources

This repository records installation sources and does not redistribute the
complete upstream repositories.

- `addyosmani/agent-skills`: MIT license, pinned in `manifests/sources.tsv`.
- `ayghri/i-have-adhd`: MIT license, pinned in `manifests/sources.tsv`.
- `karpathy-guidelines`: locally installed MIT-licensed skill, included under
  `skills/karpathy-guidelines/`.
- OpenAI curated plugins are reinstalled from the marketplace and are not
  copied into this repository.
